## Features
Explore the advanced features that will make your experience unique.

#### Monitoring
Speed, battery monitoring, total mileage, BLE, serial number, and much more.

#### Stats
View detailed graphs and save data from multiple scooters for comprehensive tracking, whether connected or offline.

#### Synchronization
Sync your data with iCloud to keep your records up to date on all your Apple devices.

#### Themeable
Customize the accent color of your control panel and the app icons to make it unique.

#### Light & Dark
All components support both light and dark color modes.

#### Responsive
Adaptable components that look amazing on **iPhone, iPad and Mac**, and on **Android phones and tablets**.

---

## Compatibility
Supports both **Official Firmware** and **CFW** on Xiaomi and Ninebot scooters.

| Brand | Firmware | Tested models |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Xiaomi** | Official and CFW (CamiBLE and more) | M365 Pro, Mi Electric Scooter 1S, Pro 2, Pro 2 Mercedes Edition, Mi Electric Scooter 3, Mi Electric Scooter Lite |
| **Ninebot** | Official and CFW (SHFW and more) | Max G30, F20, F25, F30, F40 |

Full list at [scootertools.app/compatibility](https://scootertools.app/compatibility).
